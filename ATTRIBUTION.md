# Attribution

The initial explicit binary-feature likelihood, pixel clustering, least-squares formulas, table operations, list/Boolean examples and tabular-model comparisons came from locally retained reference and filled notebooks. Shared reference material is not claimed as solely authored work. Assisted completion repairs the empty digit-test split, inconsistent thresholding, incorrect regression multiplier, noncontiguous labels, empty clusters and preprocessing leakage, with independent checks and fresh measured runs. Established algorithms are not original research.

MNIST is by Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and Christopher Burges; source: https://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/ . The UCI Car Evaluation dataset is credited to Marko Bohanec: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/19/car+evaluation . The wine-review dataset is Zack Thoutt's retained Wine Enthusiast collection: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zynicide/wine-reviews . The local bank-contact, bank-loan, Titanic and demographic tables retain their respective original source ownership; their provenance is not independently certified here. Iris, breast-cell and diabetes benchmark data is distributed with scikit-learn. No raw table, customer/passenger record, picture or fitted binary is redistributed.
